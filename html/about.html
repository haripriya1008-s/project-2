About Track & Go

Better journeys
begin simply.

We’re building a friendlier way to think about railway ticketing: clear choices, useful information, and less friction between “let’s go” and getting on board.

✦

Why we’re here

Rail travel connects people to the places and moments that matter. Track & Go is a railway ticketing project designed to make the first step—planning a trip—feel straightforward and welcoming.

◎

What we’re building

A simple journey-planning experience where travelers can enter their stations, choose a date, and select a passenger count. The current site is a visual prototype; ticket search, payment, and reservations are not connected yet.
